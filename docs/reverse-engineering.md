# Reverse engineering notes

Public pages only. No logins, no decompilation, no copied code or assets. Pages were read with Crawl4AI on 10 November 2026.

## Imersive (imersive.io)

Imersive describes a B2B fashion platform: sizing from a phone or webcam, virtual try-on marked "coming soon", generative catalogues, AI mirrors, and 3D stores. The public page argues that catalogues fail the question "how does this look on me", and quotes industry return and abandonment figures.

AuraFit keeps the in-store question and drops the reusable body scan. The shopper is in the shop. The garment is from that shop's catalogue. A photo is one session, not a stored avatar.

## Glance (glance.com)

Glance presents a phone, TV, and brand-store shopping agent: a feed, a conversation, and a path to buy from brand stores. It is a personal shopping app with an account.

AuraFit does not keep a shopper profile. Six looks come from the floor the person is standing on, and the phone is WhatsApp or the group controller, not an installed agent.

## Fynd GlamAR

`https://www.fynd.com/solutions/glamar` returned a 404 on this read. No product behaviour was taken from it.

## What was not copied

Layout, illustration, colour, and copy from these sites are not in the repo. Catalogue drawings in `public/catalog` are generated by `scripts/write-catalog-art.mjs`. Alaaya, Flickd, and StyleUAI were not fetched in this pass; nothing from them is in the product.
