# Backlink strategy (legitimate only)

Aurafit should earn links through useful content and accurate listings — not paid placement schemes.

## Do

- Publish a short technical or product write-up (how the stylist agent works, wardrobe → look pipeline) and share it on founder LinkedIn / personal sites.
- Submit to relevant startup / AI product directories that accept editorial listings (no paid “DR boost” packages).
- Keep founder profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn, personal site) pointing at the live Aurafit URL with a one-line product description.
- Invite journalists or newsletter authors only when there is a real story (launch, open-source piece, research note).

## Do not

- Buy links, PBNs, link networks, or “guest post packages.”
- Spam blog comments, forums, or social threads with the site URL.
- Cloak affiliate or doorway pages for ranking.

Revisit quarterly; quality over volume.
