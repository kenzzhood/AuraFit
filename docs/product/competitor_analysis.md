# AuraFit / Atoms Smart Dressing: competitor analysis
Prepared 9 Nov 2026 (IST). Research only: nothing was contacted, signed up for or bought.
Legend: **[V]** = verified on a live page this session (URL given). **[Claim]** = the company's own claim, seen on its page but not independently tested. **[Doc]** = stated only in Goutham's CEO strategy chat; I could not confirm it. **[U]** = unverified.

## 1. The answer in 5 lines
1. **Biggest threat:** Imersive.io is based in Chennai (Neelankarai) and already has an AI mirror live in a store (NEWME, HSR Bengaluru). Alaaya sells almost exactly our complete-look concept and is already in Salem, Tamil Nadu. Fynd (Reliance) is the big-company threat because it bundles a mirror with POS and inventory.
2. **Steal #1:** pre-render looks overnight (Breuninger's "Level 1", StyleUAI's "bulk try-on overnight"), so the first screen appears instantly. This also helps problem 3, timing.
3. **Steal #2:** a mirror-to-phone QR or WhatsApp handoff (Flickd, Roopastra, Fynd).
4. **Steal #3:** log every item that was tried but not bought, and sell that data to the retailer (Flickd: "the 98 she tried and left").
5. **Our clearest gap or advantage:** nobody publicly tells shoppers which rack or section an item is on, and nobody works with stores that have no POS. Nobody shows a touch-free, crowd-safe single-camera kiosk either. That is where AuraFit can lead.

## 3. Company profiles

### 3.1 Alaaya (by CurateU / Velvet Aura Inc.), Pune
- **What it is:** an "AI styling mirror": a 6 ft mirror with a 55" multi-touch display. It turns the store's inventory into complete, occasion-ready looks, with virtual try-on, "inventory intelligence" from real SKUs and cross-category basket growth. [V] https://www.alaaya.in/
- **How it works:** the customer stands in front of the mirror, a picture is taken, and they try on sarees, lehengas and co-ord sets "in seconds". The flow is Discover → Style → Try → Buy. It connects over Wi-Fi 6 and is billed as "plug & play, no workflow change". The site also claims "<50 ms responsive interaction" (UI only). It is touch-operated. [V] alaaya.in; Nagpur launch post https://www.linkedin.com/posts/p-m-bharadwaj-ab82aa4a_retailtech-fashiontech-artificialintelligence-activity-7506282116675002368-vY4z
- **Deployments:**
  - SSD House of Preet, Nagpur (Sep 2026) and Pune (Aug 2026).
  - Declared "Pan India" on 1 Oct 2026: Delhi, Salem, Jalandhar and Sitamarhi, covering fashion and jewellery stores. [V] https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7511286420993634304
  - Co-founder Summit Bhatia: "we assumed big-city flagship stores would be our first customers. We were wrong". [V] https://linkedin.com/in/summit-bhatia
- **Claims:** 15–20% conversion uplift and 10–20% bigger baskets. [Claim] https://www.velvetaura.us/
- **Pricing and funding:** not public. [U]
- **Strengths:**
  - It is the closest concept to ours.
  - It is already selling to tier-2 and tier-3 stores.
  - It covers jewellery as well as clothes.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Touch only.
  - No public POS integration detail.
  - No in-store location guidance.
  - No public pricing.
- **Ideas to take:**
  - The simple 4-word journey.
  - Sell into small-town showrooms, not just Chennai flagships.
  - Add jewellery and accessories to the looks.

### 3.2 Flickd, New Delhi
- **What it is:** an "identity layer" for fashion. Two photos create a photoreal avatar in under a minute, which then works on the website, the smart mirror and soon Smart Tags. [V] https://www.flickd.in/
- **How the mirror works:**
  - The shopper uploads two photos once.
  - The whole catalogue shows up as a feed on her avatar.
  - It has AI search, checkout at the mirror and a one-scan QR save to WhatsApp.
  - In beta: the mirror "talks back" about fit, fabric, what pairs well and **where to find it**, and supports voice shopping.
  - Variants that aren't on the floor can be shown.
  - Install timeline: "live in 10 days" (contract → site visit and catalogue sync → mirror by day 7 → live by day 10). [V] https://www.flickd.in/products/smart-mirror
- **Integration:** a live Shopify app and SDK. [V] The doc says "Shopify Plus live catalogue/stock/order sync"; I didn't see that exact wording. [Doc]
- **Privacy:** "DPDP compliant", servers in India, photos deleted within 24 h, and only the avatar is kept. [V] flickd.in
- **Clients:** Chique, Snitch, The Bear House and Suta. [V]
  - Snitch is in beta at selected stores (Nov 2026). Snitch has about 125–140 stores, and a wider rollout depends on results. [V] https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/flickd-takes-ai-fashion-try-on-offline-with-snitch-pilots-in-store-experience/
- **Numbers:**
  - 20,000+ avatars, 4 lakh+ try-ons, 97% SSIM. [Claim]
  - "4x tries, −40% queues, 2x staff selling" are labelled "indicative" by Flickd itself. [Claim]
- **Funding and team:** raised $250K pre-product in 2025. Eight people, with two IIT Delhi founders. [V] https://www.flickd.in/team
- **Strengths:**
  - A reusable avatar.
  - Analytics on tried-but-not-bought items.
  - A very fast install.
  - A clear privacy promise.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - It needs a phone photo upload.
  - It is still in beta at Snitch.
  - The team is tiny.
- **Ideas to take:**
  - Pitch the retailer with "your billing counter records the 2 in 100 that sell; we capture the 98 she tried and left".
  - WhatsApp save via QR.
  - A 10-day go-live promise.
  - A public DPDP page.
  - Voice answers to "where to find it".

### 3.3 Imersive.io, Chennai
- **What it is:** a Chennai B2B AI/XR retail company. Its address on its Shopify listing is 9 Casuarina Drive, Neelankarai, Chennai. [V] https://apps.shopify.com/smart-ai-tryon
- **Products:** AI Sizing, AI Mirror, AI Catalog (gen-AI photoshoots), agentic retail commerce, and 3D/XR/CGI services. Its own site still marks "AI Virtual Try-On (Coming Soon)". [V] https://imersive.io/
- **Founders:** Roshan Raju and Vedagiri Vijayakumar. [V] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/imersive-io_founders-roshan-raju-and-vedagiri-vijayakumar-activity-7394922547273920513-tcQ1
- **Store deployment:**
  - NEWME HSR Bengaluru, launched 19 Mar 2026 by NEWME co-founder Sumit Jasoria. The mirror does sizing and try-on of "multiple products in seconds" using "patent-filed software". [V] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/imersive-io_launch-aimirror-tryon-activity-7440363612612796417-4K8i
  - An AI stylist episode shows the mirror recommending complementary pieces after each try-on. [V] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/imersive-io_morethanamirror-aistylist-imersive-activity-7498718632462594048-K9Nb
- **Sizing:** works from a photo or a 10-second body scan and gives 30+ measurements.
- **Shopify pricing:** $20 a month for sizing only, then $99–$1,500 a month plus $0.24–0.33 per request. Launched 18 Feb 2026, with zero reviews. [V]
- **Idle-screen content:** the doc says the mirror shows curated content when nobody is using it for try-on. [Doc]
- **Strengths:**
  - Based in Chennai.
  - Already live in a store.
  - Sizing is a hard extra to copy.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Only one public store deployment.
  - "Software + Services ... customize" suggests it is services-heavy.
- **Ideas to take:**
  - Add size guidance.
  - Monetise idle-screen time.
  - Treat Imersive as a possible partner as well as a rival.

### 3.4 Fynd GlamAR Magic Mirror / Fynd Horizon (Reliance), Mumbai
- **What it is:** a full-length AI screen. One photo captures the shopper's body, they try the full catalogue life-size, layer tops, bottoms, shoes and accessories, get a size recommendation, then pay at the kiosk or by QR. Fulfilment is in-store, shipped, or delivered in 30 minutes. [V] https://www.glamar.io/solutions/magic-mirror
- **Hardware:**
  - High-res RGB camera.
  - Touch screen plus an optional tablet companion.
  - Under 4 sq ft of floor space.
  - Standard 6 ft display.
  - Setup in under two hours, and "catalog syncs automatically".
  - Integrations with catalogue, inventory, POS and order management: native on Fynd Commerce, API connectors for others.
  - Privacy: "photos processed in real time and not stored". [V]
- **Speed:** "Try-on to checkout in 90 seconds". [Claim]
- **Claims:** 20–50+ garments tried per session and 10–40x catalogue reach. [Claim]
- **Price:** a monthly subscription that covers hardware, software, catalogue integration and support, priced to the number of mirrors and catalogue size, with a 30–60 day pilot. [V]
- **Wider stack:** StoreOS (billing, inventory, endless aisle, clienteling), and Konnect with Tally, Odoo and ZWing POS integrations. A "multichannel smart mirror system" is on the 2026 roadmap. [V] https://www.fynd.com/blog/fynd-in-2026
- **Doc claims I could not find:** a Reliance Trends deployment going live in March 2026, 2,000 SKUs displayed vs 50,000+ available, the UNO / Ratl / JCP components, depth cameras, and a Mumbai cluster. I found no public case study. [Doc]
- **Related deployment:** GlamAR eyewear try-on is live at Vision Express, which belongs to Reliance. [V] LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/posts/fynd-shopsense_glamar-is-now-live-on-vision-express-900-activity-7466753200079925248-84gJ
- **Strengths:**
  - It owns POS and inventory for many Indian retailers.
  - It has Reliance distribution.
  - Its bundled subscription is easy for a retailer to buy.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Garment-first: shoppers build outfits themselves.
  - Enterprise focus: it won't serve a notebook-run Chennai shop.
- **Ideas to take:**
  - Include the hardware in the monthly fee.
  - Show "try-on to checkout in 90 s".
  - Let shoppers order a missing size from the screen (endless aisle).
  - Promise "photo never stored".

### 3.5 StyleUAI, Gurugram
- **What it is:** mainly a content company: InstaCollab (licensed influencer try-on posts in 10 minutes), reels, AI photoshoots, and a Shopify try-on widget. It also has a Store Try-On product. [V] https://www.styleuai.com/
- **Store flow:** the shopper picks a product on the screen, one photo is taken on the standee, and a photoreal output is produced, with the same photo reused for every outfit in the store. [V]
- **Hardware:** it runs on an existing TV with a built-in or external camera, or with no camera at all. [V] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/styleuai_styleuai-aimirror-virtualtryon-activity-7503419425791057920-bOZX
- **Staff-led pitch:** "30 second do, aap pe try karke dikhata hoon" ("give me 30 seconds, I'll try it on you and show you"). [V] https://www.linkedin.com/posts/styleuai_store-tryon-by-styleuai-activity-7481260647758864384-ng_M
- **Bulk try-on:** "Upload the whole catalogue. Try-on for every SKU, overnight." [V]
- **Clients shown:** Vero Moda / Bestseller, Bharat Reshma, Kiaasa Retail, Stitch Stories, Bespoke by Suhas and raagadesai.com. Its own claims include "3x conversions" and "2x footfall". [Claim]
- **Programmes:** an NVIDIA Inception member and shows a Google Gemini Partner badge. [V]
- **Doc claims I could not see on the current site:** 3–5 day deployment, POS/CSV catalogue import, QR purchase, a multi-store dashboard, and an AI stylist on the kiosk. [Doc]
- **Funding:** none disclosed. [U]
- **Strengths:**
  - Cheap retrofit onto an existing screen.
  - Retail clients already.
  - A second revenue line from content.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - The store product is a side line.
  - The journey starts with a garment, not the customer's needs.
- **Ideas to take:**
  - Offer an existing-TV retrofit tier.
  - Pre-render the catalogue overnight.
  - Give staff a one-line script.
  - Sell the retailer the generated images for Instagram too.

### 3.6 Roopastra, India
- **Claims on its site:**
  - Upload regular product photos with no photoshoot needed.
  - Mirror-to-phone QR handoff.
  - Touch and gesture optical sensor.
  - "Instant POS & Shopify compatible".
  - Self-checkout that reserves inventory.
  - Results in "less than a second".
  - −68% returns and 3.4x cart conversion.
  - A "multi-layer physics engine" for saree pleats and zari.
  - Analytics with foot-traffic heatmaps and engagement time.
  - [Claim] https://roopastra.com/
- **Hardware and pricing:**
  - Four models: a 32" countertop pod, a 32" standee, a 43" 4K mirror and a 55" mirror with LiDAR.
  - Installation from $499.
  - A free 30-day on-site pilot, plus per-try-on API credits.
  - [V] https://roopastra.com/pricing
- **Reality check:** its careers page is hiring a sales lead to "find and close Roopastra's first brand partners", so it has no named clients yet. [V] https://roopastra.com/about
- **Ideas to take:**
  - A free or cheap 30-day pilot.
  - Hardware tiers (counter pod, standee, full mirror).
  - A pricing configurator.
- **Caution:** treat its speed and accuracy claims as marketing.

### 3.13 Global benchmarks and failures
- **Breuninger + Google Cloud (Germany):**
  - Launched in the app on 3 Mar 2026 in all 10 countries, for selected women's and men's categories. [V] https://www.e-breuninger.de/de/medienportal/breuninger-feiert-145-jahre-am-standort-stuttgart-vogue-loves-breuninger-begeistert-besucherinnen-und-besucher-1/
  - Three levels: catalogue enrichment (offline batch), body-type selection, and "be your own model" from a selfie. [V] https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/retail/how-breuninger-boosted-sales-with-its-be-your-own-model-ai
  - Selfie preprocessing, three months from vision to launch, and a six-week A/B test that found higher conversion and contribution margin (no figure published). [V]
  - Google cut inference time with new model checkpoints. [V]
  - **Takeaway:** Google's Vertex AI Virtual Try-On API makes try-on a commodity.
- **H&M Smart Fitting Room (Sweden):**
  - RFID readers and screens; customers request sizes without leaving the room.
  - 50% of customers used the screens, 4 requests per room per day, and requested items converted 40% better.
  - Staff missed requests because notifications were unreliable, so the team shifted focus to the staff app.
  - [V] https://eidit.it/projects/smart-fitting-room. The doc's mention of COS is not verified.
- **Rebecca Minkoff / Oak Labs (US):**
  - RFID mirrors "tripled expected clothing sales" in 2015. [V] https://digiday.com/marketing/rebecca-minkoff-digital-store/
  - By Jan 2020 the stores no longer used RFID, with no reason given. [V] https://www.modernretail.co/retailers/after-years-of-hype-rfid-is-still-slowly-catching-on-in-retail/
  - Oak Labs cost about $10k per room and was sold to Zivelo in 2018. [V, from the gesture research] https://www.glossy.co/fashion/its-taking-away-one-of-the-reasons-to-go-into-a-store-why-fitting-room-tech-has-been-slow-to-catch-on/

- **Reactive Reality / PICTOFiT (Austria):** **insolvent.**
  - Bankruptcy proceedings opened in Graz on 28 Nov 2025, with €3.2M of over-indebtedness and 16 staff.
  - A sale of the whole business is planned; no restructuring.
  - Causes: post-COVID revenue drop, rising costs and failure to find investors.
  - [V] https://steiermark.orf.at/stories/3331997/
  - **The doc treats it as active, which is outdated.**
- **Vue.ai (Mad Street Den, Chennai):** a distress sale to M2P Fintech in Mar 2025 for about $10–15M, after raising more than $50M. [V] https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/funding/m2p-fintech-in-talks-to-acquire-ai-startup-vue-ai-in-distress-deal/articleshow/118704060.cms The doc's case-study figures (3M products, 51.82% of tags corrected, Pernia's 26.3%) were not re-checked. ## 7. Gaps nobody fills (publicly)
1. **Telling shoppers which rack or section an item is on inside a showroom.** The only near-misses are ORBO (mall-level navigation) and Flickd (a voice beta that says "where to find it").
2. **Stores with no POS or inventory system.** Every vendor assumes Shopify, a POS or a clean catalogue. Nobody offers "we photograph and barcode your stock, and you send a nightly sales file".
3. **A touch-free, crowd-safe single-camera kiosk** (one active user, others join by QR). Most rivals use touch; Roopastra only claims a gesture sensor; Smartiam uses Kinect.
4. **A reliable staff-side request app.** H&M learned this the hard way, and no Indian vendor shows one.
5. **Kids' wear try-on.** Perfitly excludes children, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst blocks minors, and our klein fallback covers it.
6. **Tamil and other regional-language voice or UI.** None found.
7. **The six-look, need-first flow tied to stock and location.** Alaaya and Glance are the nearest. This gap is contested, not empty.

## 8. Threats to AuraFit
1. Imersive.io is already in Chennai and live in a store, so it will pitch the same retailers.
2. Alaaya is already in Tamil Nadu (Salem) with the same idea, and it is winning tier-2 stores.
3. Fynd can bundle a mirror free inside its POS subscription for its retailers.
4. Glance + Google: an Android TV on the shop floor could become a free try-on screen.
5. Try-on itself is a commodity: Google's Vertex AI try-on API, FASHN and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst are open to anyone, so try-on alone is no moat.
6. Flickd's cross-brand avatar could become a network effect.
7. This category kills companies: Reactive Reality (insolvent), Vue.ai (distress sale), Dharpan (shrinking), Oak Labs (sold), Ultraleap (administration). Watch costs and services load.

## 9. Top ideas to steal (ranked)
1. **Pre-render looks overnight** for the catalogue on stock models, so the first screen is instant, then swap in the shopper's personal try-on (Breuninger Level 1, StyleUAI bulk). This also solves part of problem 3.
2. **QR / WhatsApp handoff** of the six looks to the shopper's phone (Flickd, Roopastra, Fynd).
3. **Log "tried but not bought"** and make it the retailer's headline report (Flickd).
4. **Fast, low-risk pilot:** live in 10 days (Flickd) with a 30-day pilot (Roopastra, Fynd). Keep it paid, as the CEO doc advises.
5. **One monthly fee that includes the hardware** (Fynd). Dharpan's ₹25k/month SaaS is a local price anchor.
6. **A staff app with alerts that actually arrive** (H&M).
7. **An existing-TV retrofit tier** (StyleUAI).
8. **Skin-tone colour ranking with a plain reason** (Vitron).
9. **Order a missing size from the screen** (Fynd's endless aisle).
10. **Ads or curated content on the idle screen** (Imersive [Doc], Try My Style).
11. **A public DPDP promise:** photo deleted within 24 h and India servers (Flickd), or "never stored" (Fynd).
12. **Sell to tier-2 and tier-3 showrooms** too (Alaaya's lesson).
